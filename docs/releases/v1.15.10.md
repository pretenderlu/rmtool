# rmtool v1.15.10

## 中文

- 修复部分设备因电源状态节点名称不同而无法使用固件管理的问题。
- 固件安装和分区切换仍要求设备接通电源且电量至少为 50%；无法确认电源状态时不会写入。

## English

- Fixed firmware management on devices that expose power status under different node names.
- Firmware installation and partition switching still require external power and at least 50% battery; rmtool refuses to write when power status cannot be confirmed.
