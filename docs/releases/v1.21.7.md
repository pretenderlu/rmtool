# rmtool v1.21.7

## 中文

- 适配 3.30 测试版：Paper Pro、Move、Pure、RM1 为 `3.30.0.108`，RM2 为 `3.30.0.109`。Paper Pro 和 Move 新增该固件的中文精确划词、微信读书安装与入口适配；五款均适配 AppLoad／KOReader。
- 修复 3.30 下扫码传书导致设置打不开、无法开启接收的问题。
- 文档中心按需开启原生导入，传完恢复原设置，不再单独初始化。Move 已实测原生导入及设置恢复，其余机型为离线验证。

## English

- Adds 3.30 beta support: `3.30.0.108` for Paper Pro, Move, Pure, and RM1; `3.30.0.109` for RM2. This includes precise Chinese selection and WeRead on Paper Pro and Move, and AppLoad/KOReader on all five models.
- Fixes QR transfer on 3.30 so it no longer blocks Settings or fails to start reception.
- Documents opens the native importer on demand and restores its previous setting afterward, with no separate setup menu. Native import and settings restoration were tested on Move; other models were verified offline.
