# rmtool v1.18.1

## 中文

- 修复 RM1/RM2 在 3.28.0.172、3.29.0.152 上原生简体中文扩展的启动兼容性问题。
- 支持识别并修复更新已安装的旧版汉化包，保留其他已验证插件和设置。

RM1 用户反馈的启动崩溃仍待实机复测。已触发启动保护的用户，请先保留保护并按 [Issue 20](https://github.com/pretenderlu/rmtool/issues/20) 的说明采集诊断信息。

## English

- Fix native-Chinese extension startup compatibility on RM1/RM2 running 3.28.0.172 or 3.29.0.152.
- Recognize and repair installed older Chinese packages while preserving other verified plugins and settings.

Hardware retesting of the reported RM1 startup crash is pending. If startup protection is active, keep it in place and follow the diagnostic instructions in [Issue 20](https://github.com/pretenderlu/rmtool/issues/20).
