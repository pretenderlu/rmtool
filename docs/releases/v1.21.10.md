# rmtool v1.21.10

## 中文

- 适配 reMarkable 2 正式固件 3.29.2.1：简体中文、拼音输入、点击翻页、原生文档上传及扫码传书。
- 该固件的「修复空间」可将已识别的霞鹜文楷备份到 /home，验证新字体配置生效后才清理系统分区的旧副本。

## English

- Adds support for Simplified Chinese, Pinyin, tap-to-turn, native document upload and QR transfers on reMarkable 2 firmware 3.29.2.1.
- On this firmware, space recovery backs up recognized LXGW WenKai fonts to /home and removes the old system copy only after verifying the new font configuration.
